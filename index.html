Sushil Anand

Ancient wisdom for a conscious and balanced life.

Sushil Anand is a spiritual master and meditation guide dedicated to making the transformative wisdom of meditation accessible to people from all walks of life. Through Anand Foundation, he is working to create a more conscious, balanced, and compassionate society through meditation, Kriya Yoga, and spiritual education.

A scholar of philosophy, psychology, Jyotish (Vedic astrology), and Ayurveda, Sushil Anand brings together ancient Indian wisdom and practical approaches to help individuals understand the mind, develop inner awareness, and lead healthier and more purposeful lives.

Our Purpose

The mission of Anand Foundation is to make meditation simple, practical, and accessible so that people can experience greater mental clarity, emotional balance, self-awareness, and inner peace.

A special focus of Sushil Anand’s work is children and young people. By introducing meditation and inner-development practices at an early age, he aims to help the next generation develop stronger concentration, emotional resilience, self-discipline, compassion, and a deeper understanding of themselves.

Through Anand Foundation, meditation education camps, online meditation programs, and other initiatives bring authentic meditation practices and spiritual knowledge into everyday life.

We believe that when individuals become conscious and balanced, families become stronger, communities become healthier, and society moves toward greater harmony.

Our goal is not merely to teach meditation, but to create a culture of inner awareness—one person, one family, and one generation at a time.

Our Meditation Programs

The path of self-realization is divided into three levels and completed step-by-step. A minimum period of six months is required to complete Level One, which includes pranayama, concentration methods, and meditation. After this, the seeker moves to Level Two, which includes meditation methods and Naad listening. After completing Level Two, one has to practice Kriya Yoga, Jyoti Mudra, and Maha Mudra in Level Three. After these three levels, continuous practice for at least three years is necessary to attain self-realization. This path belongs to the Guru-Shishya tradition, therefore only those who have the eligibility to be a disciple should join. Whosoever seekers want to proceed on this path, they should fill and send the form and they are formally given initiation by the institution.
